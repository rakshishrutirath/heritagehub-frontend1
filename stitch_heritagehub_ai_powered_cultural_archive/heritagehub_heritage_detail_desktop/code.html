Pipili Applique Work

location_on Pipili, Odisha
language Odia
category Traditional Craft

About this Heritage

Originating from the 12th century, the intricate applique work of Pipili is inextricably linked with the rituals of the Jagannath Temple in Puri. Historically, skilled artisans created vibrant canopies, umbrellas, and banners for the Lord's annual Rath Yatra (chariot festival).

The craft involves cutting brightly colored fabrics into patterns of animals, birds, flowers, and geometric shapes, which are then meticulously stitched onto a base cloth using varied embroidery techniques. The primary colors utilized are red, yellow, green, and black, reflecting the robust and energetic spirit of Odia culture.

Today, Pipili serves as a vibrant hub where this ancestral craft has evolved to include modern utility items, yet it remains a profound symbol of the region's artistic devotion and heritage, sustained by generations of craftsmen families.
