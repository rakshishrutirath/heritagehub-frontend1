Pipili Applique Work

article
Craft Language: Odia Puri, Odisha

"The applique work of Pipili is a traditional art form where pieces of colored fabrics are stitched onto a base cloth to create intricate patterns and motifs, often depicting flowers, animals, and mythological figures. Historically used to decorate the chariots during the Rath Yatra festival in Puri. The technique involves cutting, folding, and stitching, requiring immense precision."
