Pipili Applique Work

Category: Textile Craft Lang: Odia / English Loc: Puri District

Description provided by contributor:

"This is traditional applique work from my village in Pipili. We use brightly colored cloth cut into shapes of animals, flowers, and gods, and stitch them onto a darker base cloth. It is mainly used for the chariots during the Ratha Yatra festival, but now we also make umbrellas and bags."

AI Structural Translation (Draft)

"Pipili applique work, locally known as Chandua, is a traditional textile craft from Pipili, Odisha. The technique involves stitching vibrantly colored, precisely cut fabric shapes—depicting flora, fauna, and deities—onto a contrasting canvas base. Historically integral to the Ratha Yatra festival for adorning the divine chariots, the craft has evolved to include contemporary items like decorative umbrellas and bags."
